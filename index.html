The idea that ties it together

One connected context layer, not another point tool

You described the vision as a spider rather than a single direction: an orchestrator that brings many solutions together and draws insight from each. That is exactly the role Glean plays. It connects to the systems and data you already have, builds one shared layer of enterprise context on top, and lets your teams and agents work from a single source of trusted answers.

"This orchestrator should be able to integrate with other solutions that give me other insights or capabilities. It's like a spider, not one direction."

It also keeps HP's options open. Glean is model-neutral, so your teams can use the best model for each task, from OpenAI and Anthropic to open-source and Glean's own, all grounded in the same secure, permission-aware context rather than locked to any single provider.

Lead-to-Cash Processes

Bring old, unconnected quote, contract, and billing systems into one connected flow, the pillar you're still shaping.

How Glean does it

  • Connects the systems you already run. Through prebuilt and custom connectors, Glean indexes both the structured records and the unstructured documents in your quote, contract, ERP, and billing systems, with no migration or rip-and-replace.
  • Creates one view across the disconnect. A person or an agent can ask a single question that spans quote to order to contract to invoice and get one trusted, permission-aware answer, instead of chasing it across systems.
  • Automates the handoffs that break today. Agents retrieve order and billing status, draft quotes and contract summaries, flag pricing or terms discrepancies, and route exceptions, each step respecting the user's access.
  • Lets you start now, decide later. Because Glean is a layer on top rather than a replacement, HP can capture value while the longer-term lead-to-cash architecture is still being chosen, and the same context also strengthens the other three pillars.

How we'd approach it with HP

Across all four priorities, Glean is the connected context layer that makes your existing tools and any models you build work better together, rather than one more system to buy and integrate.

  • Start where value comes fastest. Lead-to-cash is the most open area, and Glean can add value across those disconnected systems right away without a rip-and-replace.
  • Build alongside, not against. On funnel and install-base management, Glean sits on top of your Databricks work as the context and orchestration layer.
  • One secure layer across everything. Glean connects your structured and unstructured data and follows each person's permissions down to the document, so answers are both complete and safe.
  • Freedom to choose the best model. Your teams use the right model for each task, grounded in one enterprise context, keeping HP flexible as the technology evolves.
